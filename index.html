AI protein design · Kraków, Poland

We design proteins that have never existed in nature

AI models can propose proteins nature never made. We build them, test them in the lab and put the results to work: with biotech partners and in our own cancer imaging and therapy programme.

Explore the technologyWork with us

Models can start with a shape, then find its sequence

Usually, a model takes a protein sequence and predicts its shape. Protein design can run the process in reverse: we search for sequences predicted to fold into a shape that grips a chosen spot on a cancer target. Then we make the proteins and test what binds. The search reaches beyond the proteins evolution has already produced.

Services and partnerships

We design binders for biotech and pharma teams, then screen hundreds of candidates together. In the lab, we measure which ones bind. The same scale lets us turn larger scaffolds into microbinders or peptides, target flexible protein regions and bring two proteins together.

See binder discovery services

Our own drug programme

Our first programme targets a marker found across several cancers. We plan to use PET to track a tiny dose of labelled binder in people. If it reaches tumours, we can develop a therapeutic version that delivers radiation to the same target.
