AI protein design

Design a protein for the job you want it to do

AI gives us more control over where a protein binds and what it does. We can design a binder that brings two proteins together at a chosen angle. Then we test what changes.

Start before the target is purified

We can start designing from a target model while the lab sets up the screen. That can save time when the target protein is hard to purify. Lab tests show which designs bind and whether they do the job.
