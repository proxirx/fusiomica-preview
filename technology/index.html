Binder discovery services

Bring us a target, an existing binder or a pair of proteins you want to bring together. We can design around the requirements of your assay, imaging probe or delivery format.

Turn larger binders into microbinders or peptides

Start with a larger protein scaffold. We design compact variants that preserve its binding motif, screen hundreds of designs in one pool and test which microbinders or peptides retain the interaction you need.

Target disordered regions

We design and screen binders against intrinsically disordered regions, where the sequence can sample many conformations.

Bring two proteins together

Recruit one protein to another using an engineered binder. We test whether it promotes formation of a ternary complex.

Select with logic gates

We develop screens around combinations of interactions. An AND gate, for example, enriches candidates that satisfy both criteria.

What do you need your binder to do?

Tell us where you want it to bind, how you plan to use it and which measurements you need. We’ll scope the screen and follow-up assays with you.
